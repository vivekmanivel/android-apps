# Expense Planner

*Expense Tracker & Planner*: a private, offline-first family expense manager and budget planner for Android, with a Windows companion that syncs over your home network. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your data is stored encrypted on your own devices.

## What it does
- **Quick add** an expense by typing, by voice or from a receipt photo, read on your phone.
- **Who spent it**: one household shared between family members' phones.
- **Budgets and plans** to see where the month is heading.
- **Backups** you control, with CSV import and export.
- **Desktop companion** that pairs and syncs over your home Wi-Fi, never through a cloud.

## Install
Download the APK from the [Android Apps page](https://vivekmanivel.github.io/android-apps/) and open it on your phone. Android 8.0 or later. A new version installs over the old one and keeps your data.
