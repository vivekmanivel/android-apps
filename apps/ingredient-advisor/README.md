# Food Ingredient Advisor

Point the camera at the ingredients list on a UK food package. The app reads the label on your phone, splits it into ingredients and traces each one back to naturally available raw materials (plants, animals, minerals, water, air, microbes, crude oil or natural gas).

## What it does
- **A verdict first**: "Avoid", "Check label" or "Great fit", with a score and the reasons grouped as Not suitable, Can't confirm and Fine.
- **Profiles** (up to 6) with diets, allergies (the UK 14, common and custom), goals and strictness; check for several people at once.
- **Nutrition panel reading** per 100g, from the same photo or a second one.
- **"How it's made"**: a visual flow from each ingredient to its raw materials.
- **Compare** 2 to 4 similar products and see which fits your goals best.
- Light and dark themes; profiles are encrypted on the phone.

This is a prototype. Information only, not medical or dietary advice: always check the label yourself if you have an allergy.

## Install
Download the APK from the [Android Apps page](https://vivekmanivel.github.io/android-apps/) and open it on your phone. Android 8.0 or later. A new version installs over the old one and keeps your data.
